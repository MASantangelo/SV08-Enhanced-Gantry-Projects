UNDER REVIEW: the linked spacers measure about 5.05 mm across (anodised aluminium), too big for the 4.992-5.000 mm bore of a 695 bearing, so they won't slide in. We're evaluating replacements. If you already have them, sand the last ~2 mm of each end (the only part that goes into the bearings) down to about 4.990-4.995 mm: spin it in a drill against 400-600 grit, or roll it under a flat block on sandpaper laid on the bench. Measure in two directions as you go, and stop when it presses into a bearing with firm thumb pressure. Don't force an oversize spacer in; that loads the bearing balls. ALTERNATIVE: a 5 mm dowel pin, 40 mm long, works in place of the spacer and its two flat-head screws. At 40 mm it runs the full width of both bearings, flush with their outer faces. Get h6/h7 (slip-fit) dowels or a cut length of 5 mm ground linear shaft (h6/g6): standard m6 press-fit dowels are 5.004-5.012 mm and will jam in the bearing just like the oversize spacers. The pulley's set screws hold the dowel in place, and the pulley sits between the two bearings. |

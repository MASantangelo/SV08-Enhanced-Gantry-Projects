| 2 | M5x40 Chicago Bolt Base | [Amazon](https://amazon.com/dp/B0FLJNY14R) | Live idler shaft in the XY joints (1 per joint). 304 stainless Chicago screw: the 5mm x 40mm barrel runs the full width of both bearings, its head on one bearing and an M4x8 Ultra Thin Flat Head on the other, so the bearings are clamped and the idler can't float. Pack of 20. The M4x4 screws in the pack aren't used: their 2mm heads don't clear the front motors, so the ultra-thin heads are used everywhere. ALTERNATIVES (not as good): an M3 internally threaded spacer, 5mm OD x 35mm (amazon.com/dp/B0D3HWQ2GF), clamped by 2x M3 x 10mm ultra-thin flat heads (amazon.com/dp/B0H61WCTBX). Or a 5mm h6/h7 slip-fit dowel / ground shaft, 40mm long: it fits, but it's held only by the pulley set screws, so the idler can float up and down under belt tension, which causes vibration. |
